I was a teaching assistant for three undergraduate courses at Bilkent University while I was an M.Sc. student (2021–2024).

| Course                               | Term                                                       |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Computer Organization                | Every fall and spring semester, Fall 2021 – Spring 2024    |
| Algorithms and Programming II (Java) | Summer term                                                |
| Programming Languages (Python)       | Summer term                                                |

My responsibilities in each course included:

- Assisting in the preparation and delivery of course materials.
- Conducting weekly lab sessions to help students put the course material into practice (in Java and Python for the two programming courses).
- Grading assignments and exams to evaluate student performance.
- Providing support to students during office hours to clarify concepts and answer questions.
