I served as a teaching assistant for the undergraduate course "Programming Languages", taught in Python, at Bilkent University during the summer term while I was an M.Sc. student (2021–2024). My responsibilities included:
- Assisting in the preparation and delivery of course materials.
- Conducting weekly lab sessions to help students put the course's programming concepts into practice in Python.
- Grading assignments and exams to evaluate student performance.
- Providing support to students during office hours to clarify concepts and answer questions.
